## Description
CiteTrack keeps your Google Scholar citations at your fingertips and turns them into insight.

TRACK
• Follow citation counts for yourself and any scholars you care about
• Beautiful citation-history charts and trends
• Home-screen widgets and iCloud sync across your devices

UNDERSTAND — Citation Insights
• Per-paper analysis (Beta): choose your representative papers and analyze each one on its own
• Who cites you — real citing sentences pulled from the literature
• Notable citers — the high-impact scholars citing your work, with their h-index and papers
• Notable venues — the prestigious journals and conferences (Nature, Science, Cell, IEEE Transactions, NeurIPS, and more) citing you
• Research directions — how your work is being built upon, clustered into themes
• Citing institutions — where in the world your readers are
• Every result is tappable to drill into the specifics

SCORE
• Score your academic impact straight from your citation footprint

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Your data stays on your device; analysis runs against our own secure service
• No ads, no tracking

CiteTrack is in Beta — we'd love your feedback.
