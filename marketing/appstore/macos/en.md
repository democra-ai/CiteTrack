## Description
CiteTrack lives in your menu bar and brings your Google Scholar citations to the Mac — with a clean unified window for everything.

TRACK
• Monitor citation counts for yourself and the scholars you follow
• Citation-history charts and trends
• Menu-bar glance + a full window (Scholars · Charts · Insights · Settings)
• iCloud sync with the iOS app

UNDERSTAND — Citation Insights
• Who cites you, with notable scholars and the venues citing your work
• Notable venues — prestigious journals and conferences citing you
• Research directions and citing institutions
• Score your academic impact from your citation footprint

DESIGNED FOR macOS
• Native AppKit + SwiftUI with a unified Liquid-Glass interface
• Sign in with Google to identify your own profile

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Your data stays on your Mac; analysis runs against our own secure service
• No ads, no tracking

CiteTrack is in Beta — we'd love your feedback.
